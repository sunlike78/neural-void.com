Core Pillars

Engineered for High-Discipline Scrum Teams

Sprints don't fail at retro — they fail mid-week when unplanned tickets quietly blow the commitment baseline.

📡 0ms Real-Time Scope Radar

Instant SWR local cache monitors mid-sprint additions with zero API lag. Visual color-coded alerts warn you when additions exceed safety thresholds (🟢 15% Healthy ➔ 🟡 25% Warning ➔ 🔴 Critical Goal Risk).

⚖️ 1-Click Smart Scope Swap

When stakeholders inject an urgent 5 SP mandate, don't break commitments — swap! In 1 click, the assistant identifies and deschedules equivalent To-Do tasks back to the backlog to restore 100% capacity balance.

🔮 Predictive Breach Forecasting

Tracks Story Points injected per calendar day and calculates the exact date when scope inflation will derail your delivery goal. Includes an interactive What-If velocity simulator.

📝 AI Retrospective Compiler

Generates executive Markdown retrospectives for Confluence and Slack. Automatically classifies scope creep root causes (Security Hotfixes vs Sales Mandates vs Feature Creep).
